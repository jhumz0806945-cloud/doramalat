## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a cinematic, immersive, and high-energy atmosphere tailored for contemporary Asian pop culture entertainment—bridging prestige Korean dramas, dynamic Chinese historical epics, subtle Japanese live-action, and vivid Japanese anime. 

### Core Aesthetic Principles
- **Cinematic Noir & Luminous Accents:** Ultra-deep, cold-tinted night surfaces allow edge-to-edge media key visuals and vibrant actor portraiture to sit center stage, highlighted by intense, neon-inspired crimson-rose and royal violet accents.
- **Glassmorphism Meets Tactile Glow:** Key functional badges and floating overlays utilize high-translucency frosted glass surfaces backed by micro-diffused directional glows, evoking premium theater hardware and luminous urban streetscapes.
- **Conversion-Driven Urgency:** High-value touchpoints—such as VIP membership escalations, episode unlocks, and primary playback CTAs—utilize high-voltage crimson-magenta gradients with pure white typography to command immediate visual focus.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for OLED displays and pitch-black room viewing conditions, eliminating eye strain while maintaining electric vibrancy and crisp visual hierarchy.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Canvas Base (`#0b0c10`):** Ultra-deep space black with a cold cyan-blue undertone. Used for global page canvas and media player backdrops.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#12141d`):** Primary elevated surface for content shelves, navigation headers, and drawer menus.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1a1d28`):** Card backgrounds, popovers, and elevated modal panels.
- **Surface Hover / Highlight (`#262b3c`):** Interactive card focus states, selected list items, and input hover strokes.
- **Primary Accent (`#ff2a5f`):** Luminous crimson-rose. Drives primary call-to-actions, playback scrub heads, and active navigation indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#7b2cbf`):** Deep electric violet. Works in concert with the primary accent to produce energetic duotone gradients and highlight special anime/fantasy categories.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#ffd166`):** Warm gold. Reserved strictly for premium VIP statuses, rating stars, and top-tier subscription callouts.
- **Text & Contrast System:**
  - **Text Primary (`#ffffff`):** Uncompromised crisp white for titles, primary buttons, and hero typography.
  - **Text Secondary (`#94a3b8`):** Muted slate for metadata, runtimes, subtitles, and release years.
  - **Text Tertiary / Disabled (`#64748b`):** Deep slate for inactive tab labels, placeholders, and watermarks.

## Typography

The typography pairing merges the geometric, modern velocity of Outfit for headings and tags with the warm, open counters of Plus Jakarta Sans for dense body synopses and metadata readability.

### Type Hierarchy Usage
- **Hero Billboard & Editorial Titles (`display-hero`, `headline-xl`):** Outfit in Bold and ExtraBold weights anchors promotional banners, cinematic title treatment replacements, and episode anchors.
- **Shelf & Rail Headers (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Crisp, compact section categorizations designed for quick horizontal eye scans across infinite scrolling carousels.
- **Narrative Synopses (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Plus Jakarta Sans offers high legibility across multilingual transliterations (Pinyin, Romaji, Revised Romanization) and tight script summaries against low-luminance backdrops.
- **Metadata Badging (`label-badge`):** All-caps, tracked letterforms tailored for micro tags (e.g., `VIP`, `TOP 10`, `4K HDR`, `DUB`).

## Layout & Spacing

The interface uses a flexible 12-column layout on desktop displays and a 4-column layout on mobile devices, engineered specifically around horizontal browsing ribbons and widescreen video ratios (16:9 thumbnails, 2:3 vertical poster cards).

### Layout Rules & Responsive Reflow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Full 12-column fluid grid with `margin: 3.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Content shelves reveal 5 to 6 vertical poster cards or 3 to 4 widescreen preview tiles simultaneously.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Carousel shelves gracefully peek off-screen to signify horizontal scrollability.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-sm: 0.75rem`. Hero carousels collapse into a 3:4 vertical presentation card with absolute-positioned action panels anchored to the screen bottom.
- **Vertical Shelf Spacing:** Content rails must maintain a strict `space-xl` separation to avoid visual crowding between competing thumbnail artworks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered surface tiers, chromatic neon ambient backdrops, and frosted glass overlays rather than standard dropshadows.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Layer (`#0b0c10`):** Base canvas depth.
2. **Surface Layer 1 (`#12141d`):** Primary structural grouping layer (e.g., sticky top navigation, bottom navigation bar, collapsed bottom sheets).
3. **Surface Layer 2 (`#1a1d28`):** Elevated cards, video details drawers, dropdown menus.
4. **Frosted Glass (Overlays & Badging):** `background: rgba(18, 20, 29, 0.72); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08);`. Used on floating player controls, persistent audio/subtitle selectors, and metadata overlays.

### Ambient Glows & Focus States
- **Card Focus & Hover:** On hover or D-pad television navigation, scale elements to `1.04x`, apply an outer border `rgba(255, 42, 95, 0.8)`, and cast an ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(255, 42, 95, 0.35), 0 0 12px 0 rgba(123, 44, 191, 0.25)`.
- **Hero Billboard Scrim:** Utilize a multi-stop gradient mask along bottom and left borders: `linear-gradient(to top, #0b0c10 0%, rgba(11, 12, 16, 0.8) 40%, transparent 100%)` to ensure typography legibility over dynamic video teasers.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a medium-rounded shape geometry (`0.5rem` standard radius), maintaining a sharp modern edge while keeping tap targets soft, inviting, and friendly.

### Element Radius Mapping
- **Standard Controls & Posters (`rounded` = 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to drama poster cards, episode card thumbnails, text inputs, dropdown sheets, and media player control bars.
- **Pill Badges & Circular Buttons (`rounded-full` = 9999px):** Applied to floating audio/subtitle chips, category pill tabs, player play/pause toggles, and metadata status badges.
- **Container Shells (`rounded-lg` = 1rem / 16px):** Applied to floating modals, subscription promotion dialogs, and video preview popovers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Watch Now / VIP Upgrade):** High-energy gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #ff2a5f 0%, #d81159 100%)`. White bold text (`Outfit SemiBold`), with a subtle inner highlight (`1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Glow effect on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Watchlist / Trailer):** Translucent background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` and white text. Hover state brightens fill to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Icon Buttons (Volume, Episode Select, Share):** 40x40px circular surface with `rgba(18, 20, 29, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, housing a 20px crisp white icon.

### Floating Badges & Metadata Chips
- **VIP Status Badge:** Solid or border-glow treatment with `#ffd166` text, deep gold-tinted dark background `rgba(255, 209, 102, 0.12)`, and subtle border `rgba(255, 209, 102, 0.4)`.
- **Localization Badges (Español Latino, Subbed, Dubbed):** Compact frosted glass capsule `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with uppercase text in `label-badge` styling and crisp white-slate contrast.
- **Quality Badges (HD, 4K HDR, Dolby Atmos):** Monochromatic slate borders `1px solid #64748b` with `#94a3b8` typography.

### Drama & Anime Media Cards
- **Poster Card (Portrait 2:3):** Features subtle corner rounding (8px). Hidden metadata reveals on hover with animated vertical slide, featuring match percentage (e.g., `98% Match` in `#00f5d4` or `#ff2a5f`), age rating, episode count, and quick-add watchlist icon button.
- **Episode Card (Landscape 16:9):** Displays progress bar anchored directly to the bottom edge of the thumbnail image using `#ff2a5f`. Unwatched portions feature a semi-transparent dark grey line.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Bar:** Elongated dark pill `rgba(26, 29, 40, 0.9)` with slate border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Leading magenta-toned magnifying icon. On active focus, the stroke blooms into a dual `#ff2a5f` and `#7b2cbf` gradient ring.

### Checkbox & Radio Controls
- **Language & Dub Selector:** Pill radio tiles displaying language options. Inactive state features dark translucent background; selected state fills with `#7b2cbf` tint, bordered by glowing crimson `#ff2a5f` with a white check indicator.

### Dedicated Video Player Timeline
- **Scrubber Bar:** 4px inactive height expanding to 8px on hover. Buffer bar in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`, played duration filled with `#ff2a5f`, terminating with a 14px circular thumb containing a concentrated crimson box-shadow glow.
